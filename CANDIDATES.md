## Selection and one refinement

O5 wins: the app celebrates exactly the disaster the vacuum is avoiding.
The insurance claim supplies a concrete consequence and a deadpan closing twist.
The perfect rubric score means it meets each criterion strongly, not that humor
is objectively perfect; the underlying robot-poop accident is a familiar premise.

Draft: I missed that spot of dog poop. Last time I tried, my app said 'whole-home coverage.' So did the insurance claim.

Refinement: replace "missed" with the more culpable "left" and cut "I tried":

I left that spot of dog poop. Last time, my app said 'whole-home coverage.' So did the insurance claim.

The final wording is original to this session. It makes the omission deliberate,
keeps the machine's voice, and leaves the consequence as the last word.
